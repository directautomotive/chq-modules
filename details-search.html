How it works: typing and Enter in Customer Name work like today. The button turns green only when the first name matches exactly and the last name starts with what you typed, so "Taya Jones" or "Taya J" lights it, "Tay" alone does not. Pressing it drops down a card with every match (Maria G shows Maria Garcia and Maria Gonzalez); nothing fills until you click a name. Gray button means no match: a new customer, keep typing. The list it searches lives in Settings › Customers. Demo names and numbers are made up.
