Where The Customer List Lives

Version 1 · Oct 9, 9:25 AM · module only, not in the app

The real Settings window from the app (version 5.211). The only new thing is Customers under Shop, right below Shop. It is the list the green search button in Details looks at. Demo names and numbers are made up.

Settings

You Shop Board More

Customers

Made from every job on the board. A new name is added when its job is saved.

How it fills: the first time, the list is built from every job already on the board (name, phone, email, address, car). After that each saved job with a new name adds one. Edit fixes a phone or address for future jobs; old jobs keep what they had. Missing phone or address shows "add" in yellow, same as an empty box in Details.
