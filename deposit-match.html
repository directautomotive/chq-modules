Deposit Match Card

Version 10 · Oct 9, 2:20 PM · module only, not in the app

Click it. A yellow row has a possible match: its whole row is yellow and its pill says Match in yellow. Click the row or the pill and the card drops down with the green Found Match job; the amount that equals the deposit is already picked (click another amount to pick that one), then press Match. A red row found nothing: click it to search the jobs or enter the details by hand. Click a green Matched pill to undo it.
