Unlocked: the three dates are ONE column, "Dates" — one box to pick up, one X, one width pill (254 pixels) — instead of three (77 / 100 / 77). The header still sorts by Date In; Days and Date Out sorts live in the chevron. Today is October 13, 2026 here. Marker: "On top" is how the app draws it today (the "!" lands on "Oct 14" when the box is tight); "Own slot" gives the marker its own 18-pixel box before Date Out; "In the arrow" puts it where the second arrow was; "After the date" sets it right of the date. Days: gray 1 to 3, yellow from 4, orange from 10, red from 20 while the car is in; always red on a total loss; light gray once it is out. Date Out: a total loss shows its pick-up date in yellow (today while storage runs); a pick-up after today is green; one dash while the car is here; one dash in Date In when there is no date. Click a date to change it; the chevron opens the color settings.
