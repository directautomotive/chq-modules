1The Core Rules

  • One shape: the pill. "The family is a circle." Straight top and bottom, a perfect half circle on each end. Buttons, boxes, tabs, search, menus' lit rows. Round buttons are full circles. Cards and windows have 22 corners.
  • On the page it floats, inside a window it sits flat. Main-page pills carry a soft floating shadow; anything inside a window or menu has none, an orange Save too.
  • Color means something. Orange = the main action or the picked thing. Green tint = adds or good. Red tint = unpaid or removes. Gray = plain. Yellow = empty, still to fill. Dark = the one main action of a section.
  • Everything that repeats lines up. Same edges, same baselines, numbers in their own fixed columns.
  • 10 between separate pills. Always. The Update / Save gap is "the spacing".
